Figure A: Heat Map Anatomy (Product Intensity, d=1)

Parameters:
  g₀ = 0.3, r₀ = 0.7, κ = 20.0

Charges:
  c_G = ∫ρ_G dg = 1.0041
  c_R = ∫ρ_R dr = 1.0041
  Λ = c_G · c_R = 1.0081

Centroids:
  μ̃_G = 0.3379
  μ̃_R = 0.6621

Mean connection probability:
  p̄ = μ̃_G · μ̃_R = 0.2237

Heat scaling (product intensity):
  H = Λ · H̄  (raw = charge × bound)
  H⃗ = H̄ / 2  (edge = bound / 2)

Total heats:
  H̄(B,B) = 0.2255
  H(Ω,Ω) = Λ² · p̄ = 0.2274  (= E[|E|] node-centric)
  H⃗(Ω,Ω) = Λ · p̄ / 2 = 0.1128  (= E[L] edge-centric)

Key formula:
  h̄(g,r) = K(g,r) · ρ_G(g) · ρ_R(r)

Under product intensity, raw, bound, and edge heats all have the same shape
(just rescaled), so the 2D bound heat h̄(g,r) is a complete summary.
